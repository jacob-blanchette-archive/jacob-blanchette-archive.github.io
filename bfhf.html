Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame

Jacob Blanchette's photographs of the Oscar Micheaux Awards and other events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame.

The Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame (BFHF) was established in Oakland, California, in 1974 by Mary Perry Smith and Margot Smith Hicks to recognize the contributions of Black filmmakers, performers, and other film professionals. Its annual Oscar Micheaux Awards ceremonies, held at Oakland's Paramount Theatre from 1974 to 1993, celebrated achievements in Black cinema. The organization also supported independent filmmaking through its Filmworks festival and an independent filmmakers competition.

Jacob Blanchette photographed events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame over several years. His surviving collection includes photographs of awards ceremonies, performances, presentations, and social gatherings. His work with the organization is also documented in Indiana University's Black Film Center & Archive, which holds photographs credited to Jacob from approximately 1982 and February 1983.

About the Collection

The Jacob Blanchette Photography Archive preserves several binders documenting activities of the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame. Two binders labeled 1987 are now represented in the working public gallery. The original photographs, printed programs, and numbered album stickers are being documented together, retaining evidence of how Jacob kept his work.

The 1987 Programs and Photographs

Binder 1 contains sixteen mounted photographic prints, a film symposium flyer, and a gala program. The flyer documents a symposium held on February 21, 1987, at Wheeler Auditorium at the University of California, Berkeley; a gala dinner and dance that evening at The Concourse in San Francisco; and the Fourteenth Annual Oscar Micheaux Awards on February 22 at Oakland's Paramount Theatre. Its photographs include portraits, a panel discussion, and a handprint or signing activity. Their individual dates, locations, and participants are still being researched.

Binder 2 includes an announcement for the February 22 awards ceremony, mounted event photographs, and film/contact materials. Some material refers to a Black film festival in Chicago in June 1987. These references make it important to distinguish the different events rather than assign every image to a single ceremony.

Jacob's photographs bring these events into view alongside the printed record of BFHF programming. Identification and digitization continue as the remaining binders are examined.

Historical Sources

Related holdings include the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame Collection at the Oakland Public Library and photographs held by Indiana University's Black Film Center & Archive. These institutional collections provide additional documentation of the organization's history and Jacob's photographic work.
