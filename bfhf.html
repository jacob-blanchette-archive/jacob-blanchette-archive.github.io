Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame

Jacob Blanchette Jr.'s photographs of the Oscar Micheaux Awards and other events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame.

The Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame (BFHF) was established in Oakland, California, in 1974 by Mary Perry Smith and Margot Smith Hicks to recognize the contributions of Black filmmakers, performers, and other film professionals. Its annual Oscar Micheaux Awards ceremonies, held at Oakland's Paramount Theatre from 1974 to 1993, celebrated achievements in Black cinema. The organization also supported independent filmmaking through its Filmworks festival and an independent filmmakers competition.

Jacob Blanchette Jr. photographed events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame over several years. His surviving collection includes photographs of awards ceremonies, performances, presentations, and social gatherings. His work with the organization is also documented in Indiana University's Black Film Center & Archive, which holds photographs credited to Jacob from approximately 1982 and February 1983.

About the Collection

The Jacob Blanchette Jr. Photography Archive preserves several binders of photographs documenting activities associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame. These materials provide a record of the organization's events and the people who participated in them, while contributing to our understanding of Jacob's photographic career.

The first binder being digitized includes photographs from the Fourteenth Annual Oscar Micheaux Awards Ceremony, held on February 22, 1987. The photographs document speakers, performers, award presentations, and gatherings associated with the ceremony. The binder also contains contact-sheet materials, including photographs associated with a Black film festival held in Chicago in June 1987.

The photographs are being digitized and cataloged while preserving their original arrangement. Research is ongoing to identify individuals, document the events represented, and establish the historical context of individual images. Additional binders will be incorporated as the collection is inventoried.

Working gallery. The gallery currently contains 35 preliminary digitizations from this binder: 27 photographic prints, seven film/contact assemblies, and a printed awards announcement. The attribution to Jacob and the association of the mounted prints with the 1987 ceremony are based on family information. Some crops, subject identifications, and rights documentation remain under review. These web images are non-generative derivatives of preliminary phone captures.

Historical Sources

Related holdings include the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame Collection at the Oakland Public Library and photographs held by Indiana University's Black Film Center & Archive. These institutional collections provide additional documentation of the organization's history and Jacob's photographic work.
