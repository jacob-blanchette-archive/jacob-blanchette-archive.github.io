The photographer

Jacob Blanchette Jr.

A working biography of an Oakland photographer and the histories represented in his collection.

A photograph of Jacob Blanchette Jr. will appear here.

Research in progress. This biography incorporates family-reported information that has not all been independently verified. Dates and details may be revised as the archive develops.

Jacob Blanchette Jr. was born in Texas in 1931 and grew up in West Oakland, California. He attended Berkeley High School and later served in the United States Air Force during the 1950s, including a posting in Alaska.

Following his military service, he attended Laney College and pursued photography professionally. His surviving work includes portraits, community gatherings, cultural events, and photographs associated with Oakland organizations.

His photographs document activities connected to the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame, the Black Repertory Group, and civic and ceremonial occasions. The collection includes photographic prints, negatives, and professional equipment. Family accounts indicate that he developed photographic film at home during much of his career.

The collection and its legacy

Jacob admired the work of photographer James Van Der Zee. Research into his photographic practice, commissions, clients, and Oakland networks is ongoing. The archive seeks to preserve his photographs while identifying the people and events they depict.
