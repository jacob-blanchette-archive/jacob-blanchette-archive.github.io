Community recreation

At the ball field and on the bleachers

At an outdoor ball field, players take turns at bat while spectators gather near the fence. In other photographs, children wearing matching athletic jerseys sit on wooden bleachers, talking and posing together.

The scenes show participation in neighborhood sport, but the youth team's identity, its particular sport, and any relationship to the ball-field photographs have not been established.
