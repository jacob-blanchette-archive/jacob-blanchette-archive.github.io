The Collections

Jacob Blanchette photographed cultural and civic life in Oakland and beyond, from film awards and theater celebrations to political gatherings and portraits. His photographs include events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame and the Black Repertory Group, as well as a 1987 gala honoring Congressman Ronald V. Dellums.

Arts & Culture

Film awards, theater celebrations, musical performances, and the gatherings surrounding them. These photographs feature performers, speakers, and guests at events connected to Oakland's Black arts organizations.

Arts & Culture

Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame

Jacob Blanchette photographed events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame, an Oakland-based organization dedicated to recognizing the contributions of Black filmmakers and performers. His photographs document awards ceremonies, musical performances, presentations, and gatherings that brought together figures from the film industry and the wider community.

Explore photographs and programs from 1987, 1988, and 1990, organized by year and historical occasion. The images provide a record of people and occasions associated with an important institution in the history of Black cinema.

Arts & Culture · 1990

Black Repertory Group

Jacob's photographs of the Black Repertory Group document the activities of an Oakland theater organization with longstanding connections to the local Black community. The surviving collection includes an album commemorating the group's 25th anniversary celebration in July 1990.

The photographs capture speakers, guests, group portraits, and moments from the anniversary festivities. Preserved together in their original album, they document a gathering marking twenty-five years of the organization's history.

Community & Civic Life

Political gatherings, public ceremonies, and civic celebrations in Oakland and the surrounding Bay Area. The photographs include speeches, presentations, and portraits of public figures and community members attending these occasions.

Community & Civic Life · 1987

Ronald V. Dellums — Twenty Years of Public Service

On May 31, 1987, a gala at Oakland's Hyatt Regency Hotel honored Congressman Ronald V. Dellums in recognition of twenty years of public service. Jacob Blanchette photographed the celebration, documenting speeches, musical performances, presentations, and portraits of those attending.

The surviving album includes more than forty photographs, together with the original invitation and commemorative materials. The collection documents a civic celebration in Oakland and reflects Jacob's work photographing political and community events.
