The Collections

Jacob Blanchette photographed cultural and civic life in Oakland and beyond, from film awards and theater celebrations to political gatherings and portraits. His photographs include events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame and the Black Repertory Group, as well as a 1987 gala honoring Congressman Ronald V. Dellums.

Arts & Culture

Film awards, theater celebrations, musical performances, and the gatherings surrounding them. These photographs feature performers, speakers, and guests at events connected to Oakland's Black arts organizations.

Arts & Culture

Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame

Jacob Blanchette photographed events associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame, an Oakland-based organization dedicated to recognizing the contributions of Black filmmakers and performers. His photographs document awards ceremonies, musical performances, presentations, and gatherings that brought together figures from the film industry and the wider community.

Explore photographs and programs from 1987, 1988, and 1990, organized by year and historical occasion. The images provide a record of people and occasions associated with an important institution in the history of Black cinema.

Arts & Culture · South Berkeley

Black Repertory Group

Founded in 1964, the Black Repertory Group grew from a church drama program into a South Berkeley theater devoted to Black dramatic work, education, and community life. Jacob Blanchette photographed occasions associated with the group.

Among the occasions represented is the group's 1990 silver anniversary celebration, with speakers, presentations, group portraits, and guests gathered around an anniversary cake.
