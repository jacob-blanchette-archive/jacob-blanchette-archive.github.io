The honoree

Twenty years in public service

Ronald V. Dellums began his elected political career on the Berkeley City Council in 1967. After winning election to Congress in 1970, he took office in January 1971, representing a district that included Oakland and Berkeley. He became known nationally for his opposition to the Vietnam War and military spending and for his advocacy on civil rights and against apartheid in South Africa. The twentieth-anniversary occasion in 1987 thus reached back to his years in Berkeley city government, before his congressional service. The U.S. House of Representatives’ historical biography provides further context.

The occasion

The 1987 gala

The surviving invitation announces A Gala Celebration in Honor of Congressman Ronald V. Dellums for Sunday, May 31, 1987, at Oakland’s Hyatt Regency Hotel. It advertised Whoopi Goldberg as a special guest and dinner music by the Ed Kelly Quintet, featuring Pharoah Sanders and Jules Broussard. These are details of the advertised program, not independent confirmation of any named person's attendance.

The photographs

Performances, presentations, and guests

Photographs attributed to Jacob Blanchette show musicians onstage, speakers addressing the gathering, a commemorative plaque being presented, and guests posing together for portraits. They give a view of both the public program and the social gathering surrounding the celebration.

The photographs were preserved with the gala invitation and other commemorative printed material. Their individual exposure dates and most participant identities remain under research; the dated invitation establishes the planned occasion, not the date of every photograph.
