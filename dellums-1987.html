Ronald V. Dellums

May 31, 1987 · Oakland

Twenty Years of Public Service

Ronald V. Dellums began his elected career on the Berkeley City Council in 1967 and entered the U.S. House of Representatives in January 1971. He became known for his opposition to the Vietnam War and military spending, his advocacy for civil rights, and his work against apartheid in South Africa. For additional context, see the U.S. House of Representatives’ historical biography.

On May 31, 1987, supporters and guests gathered at Oakland’s Hyatt Regency Hotel to celebrate twenty years since Dellums first took elected office. Photographs preserved with the celebration show speeches, musical performances, portraits of guests, and the presentation of a commemorative plaque.
