The 1987 celebration

The invitation to A Gala Celebration in Honor of Congressman Ronald V. Dellums identifies Sunday, May 31, 1987 as the date and the Hyatt Regency Hotel in Oakland as the venue. Organized in recognition of twenty years of public service, the evening brought guests together for a formal dinner, musical performances, and presentations.

The printed invitation advertised Whoopi Goldberg as a special guest and the Ed Kelly Quintet, featuring Pharoah Sanders and Jules Broussard, for the dinner music. These are contemporary program details; the identities of people appearing in individual photographs remain under research.

Jacob Blanchette's photographs

Jacob photographed the gala, producing portraits of guests and photographs of performances, speeches, and presentations. The surviving binder contains forty-one mounted color prints with green numerical stickers, along with an illustrated commemorative portrait, an invitation, a printed cover, and a business card for Jacob Blanchette Photographic Services. The business card is documented privately rather than reproduced in the public gallery.

The photographs remain in their original album context. Empty numbered positions and gaps in the sticker sequence have been retained as arrangement evidence rather than treated as evidence of missing photographs. The initial catalog distinguishes the forty-one mounted photographs from the separate printed objects.

Browse the album

This first public presentation includes the forty-one photographs and three accompanying printed objects. Each image can be opened to compare the conventional presentation crop with a resized reproduction of its album page as photographed. The images are preliminary phone captures rather than high-resolution scans; some crops, orientations, reflections, and captions require additional work.

About the sources. The event date, venue, and advertised program are documented by the surviving invitation. The photographer and identification of the gala were confirmed by the collection steward in November 2026. Individual photograph dates, participant identifications, and printed-artwork creators are not independently established.
