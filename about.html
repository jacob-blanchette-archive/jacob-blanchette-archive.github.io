About the project

About the Archive

The Jacob Blanchette Photography Archive presents the work of Oakland photographer Jacob Blanchette in its historical context. Through photographs and research, it explores cultural and civic life in the East Bay and beyond.

The Collection

The archive contains Jacob Blanchette's photographic work and materials associated with his career. Its holdings include photographic prints, negatives, albums, cameras, and other photographic equipment. Programs, flyers, and related documents are also part of the collection.

His photographs encompass portraits, performances, political and civic gatherings, community sports, festivals, and landscapes. The Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame and Black Repertory Group are among the organizations represented.

The website presents a selection from the larger surviving collection. Additional material will be introduced as documentation and digitization continue.

Historical Research

Research examines Jacob Blanchette's life and career, as well as the people, organizations, and events he photographed. Sources include census records, school yearbooks, city directories, newspapers, event programs, album materials, and family recollections.

This research informs the biography, collection introductions, and photograph descriptions on the website. The archive distinguishes documented information from tentative identifications and interpretations. Descriptions may be revised as new evidence becomes available.

Access and Use

Visitors can browse the collections and consult individual photograph records for descriptions and citation information where available. Materials on the website are available for viewing and study, but their publication does not grant permission for reproduction or other reuse. Rights and permissions are considered separately from public access.
