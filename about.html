About the project

About the archive

Preserving a photographer's work alongside the historical evidence that gives the images their meaning.

Purpose

The Jacob Blanchette Photography Archive is an ongoing effort to inventory, preserve, and interpret a collection of photographs and associated materials from Jacob's photographic career.

Preservation and access

Original prints, negatives, and material objects remain distinct from their digital reproductions. The archive uses phone photography for preliminary inventory and plans higher-quality scans for selected photographs. Original digital captures and preservation masters will be retained privately, while approved web-resolution derivatives may be published here.

Research and attribution

Descriptions and identifications will be revised as evidence becomes available. Where possible, the archive will document sources for dates, personal names, and historical interpretations. The original arrangement of boxes, envelopes, and photographic groupings will be preserved as a record alongside the new physical storage locations.

Citing the collection

Each published gallery item includes a recommended citation in its expanded record, identifying the work, its collection, the Jacob Blanchette Photography Archive, and a direct link. Photograph credits name Jacob Blanchette where supported; printed programs and other documents retain their own creator-attribution status. Dates from event programs and binder labels provide context, not verified exposure dates. Citation does not constitute permission to reproduce an image.

Rights

Public reproductions and descriptions are provided for historical research and interpretation. Attribution, privacy, and reproduction-rights questions continue to be reviewed as documentation develops; rights information for individual photographs remains under review.
