About the project

About the Archive

The Jacob Blanchette Photography Archive preserves and presents photographs of cultural and civic life in Oakland and beyond, connecting the images with the people, organizations, and historical occasions they depict.

The Photographs

Jacob Blanchette photographed portraits, performances, awards ceremonies, and public gatherings over the course of his career. His surviving work includes photographs associated with the Black Filmmakers Hall of Fame, the Black Repertory Group, and Oakland's civic and political life.

These photographs offer views of performers, speakers, public figures, and community members, as well as the gatherings surrounding formal events. Together, they provide a photographic record of cultural institutions and community life in the East Bay.

The Collection

The archive includes photographic prints, negatives, albums, and other materials associated with Jacob's photographic work. Its purpose is to preserve these materials, maintain their historical context, and make selected photographs accessible to the public.

The collection is being documented and digitized in stages. The photographs presented here represent a portion of the surviving work, with additional material to be introduced as it is examined and prepared for public presentation.

Research and Interpretation

Understanding a photograph often requires looking beyond the image itself. Printed programs, inscriptions, album materials, family recollections, and historical sources can help establish the people, places, and occasions represented.

The archive distinguishes documented information from details that remain uncertain. Descriptions and identifications may be revised as new evidence becomes available, while original materials and their historical context remain part of the collection's record.

Access and Use

Visitors can explore published photographs through collections organized around historical subjects and occasions. Individual photograph records provide descriptions, contextual information, and recommended citations where available.

Public access supports viewing, historical research, and interpretation. Reproduction rights remain subject to review, and publication on this website does not grant permission to reproduce or otherwise reuse an image.
